# Community Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ja.md)

## Our standard

RELYR welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and accessibility needs. Project spaces should remain useful, respectful, and safe.

Expected behavior includes:

- Discussing ideas and technical disagreements constructively
- Giving reproducible details without exposing private information
- Respecting different levels of experience and communication styles
- Accepting moderation decisions and correcting mistakes
- Keeping feedback focused on the project rather than the person

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized content, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, repeated disruption, spam, and abusive or demeaning language.

## Scope

This policy applies to the RELYR repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, release comments, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, repetition, and the safety of affected people.

For ordinary project moderation, contact the repository owner using the current contact method shown on the owner's GitHub profile. If content violates GitHub's rules or reporting the matter to the project is unsafe, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Do not use a public issue to disclose private personal information.
